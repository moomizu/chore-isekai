# ⚔️ Chore Isekai

**Your phone fell in the washing machine. Now you're in another world, and the Adventurers' Guild is full of… chores?**

Chore Isekai is a cozy habit tracker that turns real-life chores into guild quests. Do the dishes, take the bins out, drink some water, then spend your rewards on a little RPG: recruit companions, hunt monsters in rhythm battles, go gathering, and follow the story.

### ▶️ [Play in your browser](https://moomizu.github.io/chore-isekai/)
📱 Android: download the latest APK from [Releases](../../releases). (coming soon)

---

## ✨ Features
- **Real chores as quests.** Take quests from the board or post your own. Lilia ranks them for you.
- **Proof photos.** Snap a photo to prove a quest is done, checked by an AI or a Guild Inspector (a friend or family member who checks by email). Some quests just run on trust.
- **Rhythm battles.** Hunt monsters by hitting notes on the beat.
- **Gathering minigames.** Sparkle-tapping, basket catching, fishing and a dig-site puzzle.
- **Recruit companions** to build a party, and craft and forge gear.
- **Story mode** with a goddess who really needs your help.
- **Guild ranks** from F to S, earned over time. No rushing, no stress.
- **Library.** A focus timer with lo-fi music, a journal, and a calendar for due dates.

## 🔒 Privacy
- Your save stays **on your device**. There are no accounts and no servers. You can export a backup file in Settings.
- Proof photos only go to **the AI service you choose** (with your own API key), or to your Guild Inspector by email.
- Please don't photograph faces, yours included. The app has a cover-up brush for anything private.
- AI services only let adults (18+) create accounts. Players under 18 can use a Guild Inspector instead.
